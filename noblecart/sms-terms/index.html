Your choice

SMS consent is optional and is not a condition of account creation or purchase. Providing a phone number alone does not enroll you. When you opt in, SMS becomes your preferred channel for these messages. Email may be used when SMS is unavailable.

Message frequency varies based on your activity. Message and data rates may apply.

Cancel or get help

Reply STOP to cancel or HELP for help. For assistance or cancellation requests, email support@fineascent.org. You may receive one confirmation of cancellation. Opting out does not close your NobleCart account.

Message delivery is not guaranteed. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Only enroll a number you control, and notify us if your number changes.

We do not sell or share your mobile number, SMS opt-in information, or consent with third parties for their marketing or promotional purposes. Service providers may process information as needed to operate and deliver our messaging service. See our Privacy Policy.

Changes

Updates will appear on this page with a revised effective date. Expanding the messaging program beyond your existing consent requires a new opt-in.
